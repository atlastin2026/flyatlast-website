Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FlyAtlast collects, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Information we collect

TypeWhat, and why
Account informationYour email address, used for login and to send deal alerts and account-related emails.
Trip tracking dataThe routes, dates, budgets, and preferences you enter when pinning a trip — used to check prices on your behalf.
Payment informationWhen a charge occurs, it's handled by Stripe. FlyAtlast does not collect or store your full card number.
Location (approximate)Your IP address is used, on a best-effort basis, to suggest a default currency when you sign up. This lookup can fail silently and never blocks account creation.
Device / push notification tokenIf you enable push notifications, a device token is stored (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you deal alerts.
Usage dataBasic app usage (e.g., login streaks, referral activity) used to power in-app features like streak tracking and referral rewards.
Website visit dataAnonymous, aggregate statistics about visits to flyatlast.com (pages viewed, referring site, country, device type, and clicks on key buttons). Collected without cookies and not linked to your account.
Website demo (try a fare check)If you use the free fare check on flyatlast.com: the trip details you enter (airports, dates, budget, currency), used to run one live search; a random ID stored in your browser, used to count your free checks; your IP address, used only to limit misuse and kept as a scrambled (hashed) value for up to 48 hours; and, if you choose to unlock extra checks, your email address and whether you agreed to receive FlyAtlast tips and deals.
Shared deal links and emailed dealsIf you press Share, Email me this or Save as PDF on a fare: we store a copy of that one fare (route, dates, price, airline, stops and, for app trips, the flight times) under a random link, for 30 days, so the link works for whoever you send it to. It contains no name or email address. If you ask us to email you the deal, we use the address you give (or your account address in the app) to send that one message, and we keep a scrambled (hashed) count of requests per address and IP address for up to 48 hours to prevent misuse. Anyone with the link can see the saved fare until it expires.
Tester feedbackIf you fill in our feedback form: your answers, the device and browser you pick, and any comments you write. Giving an email address is optional and is used only to reply to you. We also keep your browser type and a scrambled (hashed) IP address to prevent misuse. We keep feedback for up to 12 months, and delete it earlier if you ask at contactus@flyatlast.com.
Proof-of-purchase documentsIf a Price Watch fee is charged and you believe you paid more when you actually booked, you may upload a booking confirmation to request a refund. This document, and any price, name, or itinerary details it contains, is stored to support that review. We read the price from the document using automated text recognition on our own systems. We delete the file 90 days after we review your request (the record of our decision stays), or sooner if you delete your account. Please cover card numbers and passport numbers before uploading.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: tracking prices and notifying you of deals.
  • To process Price Watch and pin-bundle payments.
  • To send transactional emails (deal alerts, Price Watch confirmations and results, welcome emails) and, only if you have agreed to receive them, promotional emails.
  • To operate the referral and streak reward systems.

4. Email preferences

Deal-alert and welcome emails include an unsubscribe link. Promotional tips and offers are sent only if you have agreed to them, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Transactional emails directly tied to an action you took — such as a Price Watch confirmation or result — do not include an unsubscribe option, since these communicate a real account or payment event rather than promotional content.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and trip data for as long as your account remains active. A trip you delete is removed from your view but kept in our records while your account is active, because it still counts toward your account's trip limits. Proof-of-purchase documents are deleted 90 days after we review them. If you delete your account, it and its associated data are held for 30 days, during which you may contact us to recover it, and are then permanently deleted from our systems, including uploaded proof-of-purchase documents and your customer record with our payment processor (the processor and card networks may keep transaction records as the law requires). Backups of our database may keep a copy of that data for up to 7 days after it is permanently deleted, after which it is overwritten. Any Price Watch running at the time you delete your account is resolved immediately rather than deleted outright — see Section 2 of our Terms of Service for how that works.

Website demo data: the scrambled IP counters are deleted within 48 hours and saved demo search results within 24 hours. Saved fare links are deleted about a week after they expire (30 days after you create them). Email addresses given to unlock extra demo checks are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them (see Section 6).

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's privacy

FlyAtlast is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls on our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to contactus@flyatlast.com.
