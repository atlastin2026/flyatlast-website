FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

How is this different from price alerts on other sites?

Search sites and general price alerts are built around a route and dates. They show you today's price, or tell you when it changes. FlyAtlast starts from your budget. You pin a trip with the total that makes it a yes, and we email you when a real fare fits it, confirmed on two checks in a row, with the exact flight. We don't cover every site or fare, and our prices can differ from other sites, so every deal links to Google Flights for a quick compare.

How does FlyAtlast work?

Pin a trip with your route, dates and budget. We check real, bookable fares from the day you pin until you fly (25 checks per trip, closer together as departure nears). When one comes in under your budget, we send you the exact flight and a link to book. Deal alerts arrive between 8:00 and 21:00 your time.

What's free, and what costs money?

Your first 3 trips are free: 25 checks each, deal alerts, no card. More trips are US$3 for 3, and you can earn extras through referrals and a 7-day streak. Price Watch is optional, and your first one is a free 24-hour trial (one per account, no card, and we tell you what would have happened).

What's Price Watch?

It's for when you've found a fare but can't book yet. We check it every 2 hours for 48 hours (US$5.99) or 5 days (US$9.99) and email you if it moves US$5 up or US$20 down. If it ends that far up or down, you pay nothing. If it ends inside that range, the fee applies. You can start one on a pinned trip once its price is at or under your budget and departure is at least 24 hours away.

What if I pay for Price Watch and still end up paying more for my ticket?

Two things have your back. Most outcomes cost you nothing: the fee is only charged if the price ends inside our range. If it ends US$5+ higher or US$20+ lower than where you started, the card hold is released.

And if you were charged and still paid more, send your booking confirmation within 48 hours of your result. If it shows you paid more than your starting price, we'll refund the fee after a quick review.

What Price Watch can't do is hold the fare or seats, or stop an airline changing its price. What you get is 48 hours or 5 days of checks every 2 hours, plus a heads-up if the price moved a lot.

Do you hold seats or predict prices?

No to both. Airlines can change a price any time, and you'd pay the new price when you book. We don't guess where fares are heading. You see real checks, and we tell you when one meets your budget.

Why can prices differ from other sites?

Airline prices move around, and ours come from our airline data partner, so they can differ from what you see elsewhere. That's why every deal has a one-tap Google Flights link: check it before you book.
