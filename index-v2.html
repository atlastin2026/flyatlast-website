FAQ

Quick answers.

1

How does FlyAtlast work?

Pin a route, dates and budget. We keep checking until you fly. Under budget? You get the flight and a link to it.

2

What’s free?

Your first 3 trips: 25 checks each, no card. Need more? 3 extra trips for US$3.

3

What’s Price Watch?

Optional. Checks a trip every 2 hours for 48 hours (US$5.99) or 5 days (US$9.99). Your first one is free.

4

Paid more anyway?

We refund the Price Watch fee. Just send your booking confirmation within 48 hours.

5

Do you hold seats?

No. We don’t hold seats or fares. We check real fares and tell you when one fits.

6

Why do prices differ?

Our fares come from an airline data partner, so they can differ from other sites. Every deal links to Google Flights.
