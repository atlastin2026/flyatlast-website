About FlyAtlast

FlyAtlast checks flights against your budget and emails you when a fare fits.

Why we built FlyAtlast

You check a fare on Monday and it’s too high. You check again on Tuesday, and it’s still too high. Then it dips under your budget at 2 am and is back up before breakfast, and nobody tells you.

FlyAtlast does the checking for you. You set your budget once, and we email you when a fare fits it.

Example: Toronto → New York, one week

How we differ from a price alert

Many price alerts follow a route and dates, and email you when the price changes. They don’t know your budget, so you still work out whether a change matters. Ours starts from your budget and tells you when a fare fits it.
