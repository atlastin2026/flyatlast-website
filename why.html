Why FlyAtlast exists

FlyAtlast · October 2026

Booking a flight shouldn't feel like a part-time job. But if you've ever tracked a fare, you know how it goes.

The price you wanted came and went

You check a trip on Monday. Too high. Tuesday: still too high. Then you sleep, the fare dips under your number at 2 am, and it's back up before breakfast. Nobody told you. The only way to catch it was to be awake.

Checking by hand doesn't scale

Refresh, compare, repeat. Nineteen checks later you're not sure what a good price even is, and you still have a tab open for it.

"Prices dropped" isn't an answer

Most alerts tell you something moved. They don't say which flight, whether you can book it, or whether it fits what you were willing to pay. So you click through, hunt for it, and find it's gone.

Found it, can't book yet

Sometimes you've found the right fare but you're waiting on a payday, a partner or a decision. Then you worry it'll jump while you wait.

So we built it the other way round

You set the budget. We check real, bookable fares and send you the exact flight when one comes in under it. If you're not ready to book, Price Watch keeps an eye on the fare, and you only pay if the price held.

What we don't do

We don't hold fares or seats, and we don't guess where prices are heading. You see real checks, and we tell you when one fits.
