Price Watch

Found it, but not ready to book? We'll watch it for you.

Every 2 hours, for 48 hours or 5 days. If the price moves a lot, you'll know fast, and you won't pay for the watching.

  • We check so you don't. About 24 checks over 48 hours, or 60 over 5 days, overnight included.
  • Big move? You hear within 2 hours. If the price rises US$5+ or falls US$20+ from where you started, we email you right after the check that caught it, so you can compare and book, or relax.
  • You only pay if the price held. If it ends US$5+ higher or US$20+ lower than where you started, you don't pay anything.
  • Paid more when you booked? Send your confirmation within 48 hours of your result and we'll refund the fee.
