Questions

How does FlyAtlast work?

You add a route, dates and a budget. We keep checking the fares until you fly. When one is under your budget, we email you, and the full itinerary is in the app.

What does it cost to use FlyAtlast?

Track up to 3 trips at once, 25 checks each. Your first 24-hour Price Watch is included. Remove a trip or let it expire to open up a slot.

What is a Price Watch?

Found a deal but need time to check a few things? We keep watching the price for you, every 2 hours, so you don’t have to. If the price moves outside your range, you don’t pay.

What happens if I pay more after a Price Watch?

If you paid a Price Watch fee, then booked for more than the price we tracked, you get a full refund. Send your booking confirmation within 48 hours of the result.

Where do I cancel a Price Watch or manage payments?

In the app, open the Price Watch and choose Cancel watch. You can cancel within 12 hours of a 48-hour watch or 24 hours of a 5-day watch, and we release the hold on your card. Your payment method is under Payments in the app.

Do I pay FlyAtlast when I book?

No. You pay the airline or booking partner their price. FlyAtlast only charges for Price Watch.

Do you hold seats on flights?

No. We don’t hold seats or fares. We check fares and tell you when one fits.

Why do prices differ from other sites?

Our fares come from an airline data partner, so they can differ from other sites. Prices also move through the day and the week, so the same trip can cost more or less an hour later.

How can I get more trips?

Refer a friend: when they join, you each get an extra trip. Check in 7 days in a row for another. Earned trips stay yours, and a missed day restarts the streak.
