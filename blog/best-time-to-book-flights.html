Best time to book flights: what anyone can and cannot tell you

FlyAtlast · September 2026

You will see confident rules everywhere: book on a certain weekday, book exactly so many weeks out. They sound precise, but fares move by route, season and demand, and no single rule fits every trip.

What is broadly true

Popular dates often cost more. Waiting until the last minute on a busy route is risky. Beyond that, the timing of a good fare is hard to know in advance.

A practical approach instead

  • Set your budget. Decide the total that makes the trip a yes.
  • Start tracking early. More checks over more time means more chances to catch a fare that fits.
  • Book when a real fare fits. If it is a trip you do not want to miss, do not wait for a better one.
