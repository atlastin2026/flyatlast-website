Best time to book flights: what anyone can and can't tell you

FlyAtlast · September 2026

Book on a Tuesday. Book exactly six weeks out. The rules sound precise, but fares move by route, season and demand, and no single rule fits every trip.

What’s broadly true

Popular dates often cost more. Waiting until the last minute on a busy route is risky. Beyond that, the timing of a good fare is hard to know in advance.

A practical approach instead

  • Set your budget. Decide the total that makes the trip a yes.
  • Start tracking early. More checks over more time means more chances to catch a fare that fits.
  • Book when a fare fits. If it's a trip you don't want to miss, don't wait for a better one.

Why FlyAtlast doesn't predict prices

Guessing where fares will go isn't something we can promise, so we don't do it. We check bookable fares and tell you when one meets your budget, and show you the itinerary in the app.

Know your number, then let us watch. Pin a trip with your budget and we alert you when a fare fits it.
