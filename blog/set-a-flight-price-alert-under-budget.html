How to set a flight price alert under a budget

FlyAtlast · September 2026

A basic alert tells you a price changed. A budget alert tells you when a fare is one you would actually book. That is the version worth setting.

1. Choose a total for everyone travelling

Think in whole-trip terms: the most you would happily pay for all passengers together. A single number is easier to act on than a moving chart.

2. Enter the exact trip

Use the route and dates you would really book. If you can shift by a few days, allow it. Flexible dates give a tracker more chances to find a fare that fits.

3. Say what matters

Decide whether you want the cheapest fare with stops, or direct flights only. The right alert reflects your real trip, not just the lowest number.

4. Make sure the alert names the flight

"Prices dropped" is not enough. You want the flight, times, stops and fare type, plus a quick way to compare before you book.

5. If nothing fits

A budget can be too low for the route or season. If nothing hits it, widen your dates or adjust the number rather than waiting indefinitely.
