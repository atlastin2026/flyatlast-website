How to avoid overpaying for flights

FlyAtlast · September 2026

No habit can promise the lowest fare. But a few simple ones make overpaying a lot less likely.

Know your number before you search

Look at a handful of fares once, then decide the total you'd happily pay. Without a number, every price looks either too high or like a bargain.

Compare the real total

Bags, seat selection and fare rules change what a ticket really costs. A cheap basic fare can end up dearer than a slightly higher one that includes what you need.

Give your dates some room

Moving a day or two, or flying from a nearby airport, can change the price noticeably on some routes. Flexibility gives you more options, not less.

Don't book under pressure

"Only a few seats left" messages are designed to hurry you. Check your budget first, and book when a fare fits it.

Let something watch for you

A fare tracker checks while you're busy and tells you when a real fare meets your budget, so you aren't relying on luck or constant refreshing.

Do these and you'll usually feel good about the price you paid, even if it wasn't the absolute low.
