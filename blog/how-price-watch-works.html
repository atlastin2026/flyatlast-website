How Price Watch works, and when it is worth it

FlyAtlast · September 2026

Sometimes you find a fare you like but cannot book yet. You are waiting on a decision, a payday or a travel partner. Price Watch keeps an eye on that fare while you decide.

How it works

You can start a watch on a pinned trip once its tracked price is at or under your budget and departure is at least 24 hours away. We check the price every 2 hours for 48 hours (US$5.99) or 5 days (US$9.99), and we email you if it rises US$5 or more, or falls US$20 or more, from where you started. A card hold is placed at the start, and nothing is charged until the result.

How you are charged

If the price ends US$5+ higher or US$20+ lower than where you started, the hold is released and you pay nothing. If it ends inside that range, the fee is charged. Paid more when you booked? Send your booking confirmation within 48 hours of your result. If it shows you paid more than your starting price, the fee is refunded.

When it is worth it

  • Your trip's price is at or under your budget, but you cannot book for a day or more.
  • You want to know quickly if the price moves a lot.
  • You would rather be told than keep checking.

When to skip it

If you are ready to book, just book. Price Watch does not hold the fare or seats, and it cannot stop an airline changing its price.

Try it free first

Your first Price Watch is a free 24-hour trial. No card is needed, and there is one per account.
