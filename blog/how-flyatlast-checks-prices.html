How FlyAtlast checks prices (the 25 checks, explained)

FlyAtlast · September 2026

When you pin a trip, we check real, bookable fares for it. Each trip gets 25 checks, and we space them out on purpose.

Why 25, and why spaced out

Checks are spread across the time before departure, closer together as the trip gets nearer. That puts more of them in the stretch where decisions usually happen, instead of spending them all in the first week.

What we look for

We are looking for a real fare that comes in under your budget. Small up and down moves are not what you asked about, so they do not trigger an alert.

What you receive

When a fare fits, we confirm it and send you the exact flight: flight numbers, times, stops and fare type, how far under budget it is, and a link to book. Every deal also has a one-tap Google Flights link so you can compare first. Deal alerts go out between 8:00 and 21:00 your local time.

What we do not do

We do not hold fares or seats, and we do not predict where prices are going. Our prices come from our airline data partner and can differ from other sites, which is why the compare link is there.

Different from Price Watch

Price Watch is a separate, optional feature for a fare you have already found. It checks every 2 hours for a short window.

FlyAtlast does this for you. Pin a trip with your budget, and we check real, bookable fares and send you the exact flight when one comes in under it. Free for 3 trips, no card needed.
