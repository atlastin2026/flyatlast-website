How FlyAtlast checks prices (the 25 checks, explained)

FlyAtlast · September 2026

When you pin a trip, we check real, bookable fares for it. Each trip gets 25 checks, spread from the day you pin until you fly and packed closer together as departure nears.

Why 25, and why spaced out

Checks are spread across the time before departure, closer together as the trip gets nearer. That puts more of them in the stretch where decisions usually happen, instead of spending them all in the first week.

What we look for

We are looking for a real fare that comes in under your budget. Small up and down moves aren't what you asked about, so they don't trigger an alert.

What you receive

When a fare fits, we confirm it and email you the price and how far under budget it is. Open the app to see the full itinerary: flights, times, stops and fare type.

What we don't do

We don't hold fares or seats, and we don't predict where prices are going. Our prices come from our airline data partner and can differ from other sites, so it's worth comparing before you book.

Different from Price Watch

Price Watch is a separate, optional feature for a fare you have already found. It checks every 2 hours for a short window.

That's the whole trick: patient, honest checking, and a chance to compare before you book.

FlyAtlast does this for you. Pin a trip with your budget, and we check real, bookable fares and alert you when one comes in under it, with the full itinerary waiting in the app. Your first 3 trips are free, no card needed.
