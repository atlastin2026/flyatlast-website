2. Price Freeze

Price Freeze is an optional, paid feature. For a flat fee (currently $9.99 USD, converted to your account's currency at checkout), FlyAtlast will:

  • Track your selected price for 5 days from the moment you freeze it.
  • If our tracked price for that route rises by $5 USD or more above the price you froze during that window, refund your fee automatically.
  • If the tracked price holds steady, rises by less than $5 USD, or drops by less than $20 USD during that window, retain the fee — this is what compensates FlyAtlast for the days of active tracking regardless of outcome.
  • If the tracked price drops by $20 USD or more during that window, also refund your fee automatically — at that point, the freeze didn't end up helping you, so there's nothing to keep it for.
