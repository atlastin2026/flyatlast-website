1. What FlyAtlast does

FlyAtlast tracks flight prices you choose to follow ("pins") and notifies you when a price meets your stated budget. FlyAtlast is an informational and price-monitoring tool — it is not a travel agency, and it does not sell or issue flight tickets. Prices shown reflect what we found during our periodic checks and may not match the price available if and when you book through a third-party provider.

2. Price Watch

Price Watch is an optional, paid feature that monitors one of your tracked trips more closely for a set period. It is available once a trip's tracked price is at or under your budget and its departure is at least 24 hours away. Two durations are offered: 48 hours for $5.99 USD, or 5 days for $9.99 USD. The fee is converted to your trip's currency at the exchange rate on the day you start the watch, and the converted amount is shown before you confirm. Fees do not include GST/HST or any other applicable taxes, which are added on top where they apply.

Free trial

Each account may use one free Price Watch trial. The trial runs for 24 hours, requires no card, and is never charged regardless of how the tracked price moves. It otherwise works the same as a paid watch — the same 2-hourly checks and the same comparison described below — except the result is informational only. Variants of the same email address, such as adding a "+tag" to it, do not qualify for an additional trial.

What happens when you start a Price Watch

  • We record the tracked price at that moment (your "starting price") and place a temporary authorization hold for the fee on your card. This is not a charge. (The free trial places no hold, since nothing is ever charged.)
  • We check the price for that trip about every 2 hours for the length of the watch. We stop checking 12 hours before departure; if that comes before the watch would normally end, the watch ends at that point instead.

How the result is decided

When the watch ends, we compare the live tracked price to your starting price:

  • If it is higher by $5 USD or more, or lower by $20 USD or more (each converted to your trip's currency), the hold is released and you are not charged.
  • Otherwise, the held fee is charged. The fee pays for the monitoring provided during the watch.

Only the comparison at the end of the watch decides the result. Price movements during the watch that reverse before it ends do not change the outcome.

Booking-price refund

If your fee was charged, you may submit one booking confirmation per Price Watch within 48 hours after it ends. We review it and, if it shows you paid more than your starting price, we will refund the full fee. Documents that are unclear, for a different trip or in a different currency may take longer or be declined.

Cancellation

You may cancel a 5-day Price Watch within 24 hours of starting it, or a 48-hour Price Watch within 12 hours of starting it. A cancelled watch releases the hold and is not charged. The free trial cannot be cancelled through the app, since nothing is ever charged on it.

What happens once a Price Watch ends

Once a Price Watch reaches a result — including a free trial — the trip it was tracking stops being monitored and is removed from your active pins, freeing up a tracking slot. If a fee was charged, the trip remains visible for up to 48 hours so you can submit a booking-price refund request as described above; otherwise it moves immediately into your trip history. A trip that has finished a Price Watch cannot be edited or restarted — you would pin it again as a new trip.

What Price Watch is not

Price Watch does not reserve, hold, or purchase any seat or fare, and it does not guarantee that any price will be available when you book through a third-party site. If the price rises, you would pay the higher price when you book. FlyAtlast's responsibility in connection with a Price Watch is limited to the fee described in this section.
